# Code of conduct

Vecgra's community should be rigorous, curious, and welcoming. Treat other
people's time and perspective with respect, disagree with ideas rather than
people, and give technical criticism with enough evidence to be useful.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's
private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is incompatible with this standard.
Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. All
reports will be reviewed with discretion; conflicts of interest should be
declared and handed to an uninvolved reviewer whenever possible.
